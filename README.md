# execution-genesis-offer

This agent is a base. Once you have done it your way, tell your squad "update the agent to do it like this."

Agent 1. It asks you 5 questions, finds who already sells to your buyer and what they charge, reads the
3 winners whole, digs up your buyer's own words, and writes your offer: `squad/business.md`.

**What lands on the page.** 14 sections, every line with its source.

1. The sentence a stranger could repeat, your buyer, his problem in his own words, what he pays other
   people now, the kind of demo you will build, your promise with its day, and 1 price.
2. Up to 10 buyer quotes with their links, and every seller you were checked against, with its price.
3. **The winners:** the 3 sellers he already pays, read whole. What each one sells, its page top to
   bottom, its proof, its terms, and the sentence it uses for his problem.
4. **What he gets:** every deliverable of your offer, 5 to 12 lines, each one traced to the winner that
   sells it or to your own answer.
5. **The build:** how you make it, step by step, the tool named in every step, the days adding up to
   your promise, every tool's price read off its own pricing page today, and what delivering 1 costs you.
6. **Objections:** the 5 things he says before he pays, each answered off this page.

Agent 2 copies the winners' shape into your demo. Agent 3 reads the objections into your call.

**Install.** Installed with the one line on aichrislee.com/free. Paste your Apify token line (below)
before you quit and reopen Claude Code.

**Apify, once.** The dig reads Reddit and YouTube comments through Apify. Make a free account at
apify.com (no card, $5 of use free every month), copy your token from
console.apify.com/settings/integrations, and tell Claude Code:

"Here is my Apify token: ____. Use it for execution genesis offer."

Quit Claude Code, open it again in the same folder, choose to use the apify server when it asks, and
say hi. Keep the token to yourself, like a password.

**Run it.** `/execution-genesis-offer`. Answer the 5 questions in 1 message. Change any line by telling
it what the line should say. Next: `/execution-genesis-demo`.

It never sends anything, never invents a quote, and never prices past your number.
